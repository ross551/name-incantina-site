Naming Brief Rules & Terms

Last updated: November 20, 2026. These are the same rules and terms shown in the app (Your Tab, Official Naming Brief Rules & Terms). Name Incantina is operated by Goalie IP, Inc. and is for people 18 and over.

Free to play

Naming Briefs are skill-based. There is no purchase or entry fee. Points are earned by taking part; they can't be bought or exchanged for money.

The names you pour

Once you pour (submit) a name, you permanently give up any claim to it, whether or not it is selected. That includes any claim of ownership, earlier use (common-law priority) or implied contract.

Why that costs you nothing

Thinking of a name doesn't make it yours. Trademark rights come only from using a name to sell goods or services, or from a trademark application backed by a real plan to use it. So pouring, judging or being selected gives no one ownership of a name: not you, not the client and not Name Incantina. The client, and other creators, are free to clear, register and use any name poured here.

Don't register names you see

For 24 months, you agree not to register a domain name or trademark for any name poured or shown in a brief.

Creative Fees and payments

1st, 2nd and 3rd place each earn the Creative Fee for that place. Creative Fees are credited to your Tab in U.S. dollars. To be paid, you connect a payout account through our payment partner, Stripe, which verifies your identity and collects your tax details. On the 1st of each month, a balance of $25 or more is paid to that account; less carries over. Creative Fees are payment for your services as an independent creator, not an employee. Stripe collects your Form W-9 details and sends a 1099-NEC to creators paid $2,000 or more in a calendar year. Before a selected name's Creative Fee is paid, its creator accepts a short name assignment and originality warranty (shown in the app), which hands the name to the client. House briefs (run by Name Incantina, with no client) pay awards in points only; points have no cash value and can't be cashed out.

By entering this name I acknowledge I have no rights to this name, or its use, trademarks only vest through use, any residual rights I hereby grant to Goalie IP, Inc.

Name Assignment and Originality Warranty

Accepted at every pour, and again for each selected name before its Creative Fee is paid.

1. Assignment

For each of your names that a client selects, and in exchange for the Creative Fee (or points award) for that place, you assign to the client who posted that brief (for briefs posted by Name Incantina, to Goalie IP, Inc.) all right, title and interest you have or may have in the name, worldwide, including any trademark or common-law rights and the goodwill that goes with them. You will not challenge the client's use, registration or ownership of the name.

2. Originality

You confirm that you came up with the name yourself, that you did not copy it from someone else's brand, product or company, and that, as far as you know, it does not infringe anyone else's rights. A GoalieIP score is a screening estimate, not trademark clearance.

3. Hands off

You will not use or register the name, or anything confusingly similar to it, as a trademark, business name, domain name or social media handle, and you will not help anyone else do so.

4. Paperwork

If the client needs a signed document to record this assignment (for example with a trademark office), you will sign a short confirmation on request at no extra charge.

5. Payment

The Creative Fee is your full payment for the name and this assignment. It is paid through our payment partner, Stripe, once your payout account is verified. Stripe collects your tax details (Form W-9). Creative Fees are payment for services: you are an independent creator, not an employee, and you are responsible for your own taxes.

6. Moral rights

To the extent the law allows, you waive any moral rights or rights of attribution in the name.
