Delete your Name Incantina account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Name Incantina and sign in.
  2. Tap Your Tab (bottom right).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm.

By email

If you can't get into the app, email ross@goalieip.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your profile, sign-in, handle, votes, chips, notification tokens and unclaimed winnings are permanently deleted. Names you poured stay with the tables they were poured at (you gave up any claim to them when you poured), with nothing linking them to you.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Records of prize payments already made, as tax and accounting law requires (generally up to 7 years). Our payment processor, Stripe, keeps its own records under its policies.
  • Winning names already delivered to a table's host stay with that host.
