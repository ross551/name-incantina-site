Privacy Policy

Name Incantina · Last updated: November 20, 2026

Name Incantina ("we", "us") is a crowdsourced brand-naming service for Android and iOS: clients post Naming Briefs and creators submit names. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored encrypted by our sign-in provider), display name and public handle.
  • App activity: the names you pour, your optional pitches, scores, votes, points, places and Creative Fees earned.
  • Payout details (creators of selected names only): when you request a payout, our payment processor collects what it needs to pay you and meet tax rules (for example name, bank details and tax ID). We don't store bank or card numbers ourselves.
  • Device and ad data: when ads are on, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and ad interactions.

How we use it

  • To run the service: score names, run naming briefs and votes, credit points and pay Creative Fees.
  • To check names: names you pour are sent to our scoring services (see below) to review them against naming rules and the U.S. trademark register.
  • To keep Name Incantina fair and safe, prevent fraud and enforce the brief rules (in the app under Rules).
  • To show rewarded ads that earn you points.
  • To keep the app working: if the app hits an error, it sends us an error report (the error, the app version and your phone type).
  • To send notifications you allow (for example when results are in), using a push token for your device.

We don't sell your personal information for money.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) and GoalieIP receive the names you pour, and the naming brief, to score them. They don't receive your email.
  • Google AdMob serves ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Stripe processes client payments and payouts to the creators of selected names.
  • Clients who post a brief see the names poured for their brief, your pitch and your handle. Names you pour may be used by the client, as described in the brief rules.
  • The public: on open briefs, selected names and the handles of their creators may be published. Other names stay private.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Ads and your choices

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. If you're in a U.S. state with a privacy law, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information for ads. You can change either choice in the app, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your account while it's open. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Your Tab, Delete account) or on the web, or by emailing us at the address below. Deleting your account removes your email, handle, points and history. We may keep records we need for payouts, taxes, fraud prevention or the law. Names you already submitted stay with their briefs, with no link to you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, to object to or limit how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Email us to use these rights. We won't treat you differently for using them.

Children

Name Incantina is for people 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and reputable service providers. No system is perfectly secure.

International transfers

We're based in the United States and our providers may process data in the U.S. and other countries, under appropriate safeguards where the law requires them.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
