You are buying a commercial brand-naming service: creators develop names for your brief, and the IP rights in each name you select are assigned to you. Creative Fees are paid in full to the creators of the selected names; the naming service fee covers the service.

You pay on Stripe's secure page (card, Google Pay or Apple Pay). Once payment clears, the house takes a quick look, usually within a few hours, and the last call clock starts when the brief opens. If we can't approve it, you get a full refund. If no name clears your bar, the Creative Fee pool is refunded (the naming service fee is kept).

Name generation only

  • Name Incantina is a name generation service. Creators suggest names; we don't give legal advice.
  • Names developed with Name Incantina are not guaranteed to be available: for trademark registration, as a domain, or for use in business.
  • GoalieIP ratings are a screening estimate, not a trademark search or clearance opinion.
  • There is no guarantee of a name's effectiveness, in marketing, sales or otherwise.
  • Before you use or file a selected name, have it cleared by a trademark professional.
